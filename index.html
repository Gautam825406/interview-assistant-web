FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an API key?

No. The AI runs on our servers — you just sign up and use it. There is nothing to set up and no key to paste anywhere.

How does live listening work?

In Chrome, use your microphone or type the question. For Meet or Zoom in the browser, share the tab with audio. Typed questions always work as a fallback — and on the desktop app you can use system audio too.

Is it invisible in screen-share?

No — the web version is NOT invisible. If you share the whole screen or this browser window, it will show. Only the native desktop app can hide from screen-share. Use live help only where it is permitted.

Where is my data?

Your account, plan and usage live on the service. Your session notes and resume stay in your browser unless you save them in a session.

How do I pay?

Pick a plan on the Pricing page and pay securely online — UPI, cards or net-banking. Your plan activates as soon as the payment succeeds.

What happens when my free sessions run out?

The free plan includes 10 live sessions and 1 mock interview. When they're used up, upgrade to a paid plan for unlimited sessions — your saved sessions stay where they are.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Settings — you keep access until the plan expires, and you can come back whenever you like.
