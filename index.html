FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it need an API key?

No — the AI runs on our servers. You just sign up and use it; the service owner manages the AI providers.

How does live listening work?

In Chrome, use your microphone (SpeechRecognition) or type the question. For Meet/Zoom in the browser, share the tab with audio. Typed questions always work as a fallback.

Is it invisible in screen-share?

No — this web version is NOT invisible. If you share the whole screen or this browser window, it will show. Only a native desktop app can hide from screen-share. Use live help only where it is permitted.

Where is my data?

Your account, plan and usage live on the service. Your session notes and resume stay in your browser unless you save them in a session.

How do I pay?

Pick a plan on the Pricing page and pay securely with Cashfree (UPI, cards, net-banking). Your plan activates as soon as the payment succeeds.
